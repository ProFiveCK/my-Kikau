Made for cautious Mac maintenance

A cleaner should show its work. myKikau keeps the user in control, avoids system files, and logs what it does locally.

👁️

Preview before delete

Review file paths, sizes, and protected items before anything moves to the Trash.

🧹

Leftover-aware uninstall

Removes the app bundle plus LaunchAgents, login items, and related files in your Library.

📊

Live system dashboard

CPU, memory, disk, network, GPU, thermals, and battery metrics refreshed every two seconds.

🧬

Duplicate finder

Content-hash verification helps identify real duplicates before you decide what to remove.

📦

Large-file review

Find big items across Downloads, Documents, Desktop, Pictures, and Movies.

📝

Local activity log

Every action is written to ~/Library/Logs/myKikau/operations.log.

Built to be trusted with your Mac

myKikau is deliberately conservative: transparent previews, Trash-first deletion, and guardrails around critical apps.

Safety guardrails

myKikau avoids macOS system files and protects critical Apple bundle IDs including Finder, Dock, and System Settings. It also recognises endpoint-security agents such as CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, and Jamf so managed Macs stay protected.

What to expect

  • Full Disk Access is requested and explained on first launch.
  • Cleanup and uninstall actions are previewed before execution.
  • Files are moved to Trash rather than silently destroyed.
